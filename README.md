# Night crossing

A tiny offline interactive poem: a ferry, five moments, and a fjord turning from midnight to morning.
An own-project play pass, not another dashboard. Original words and hand-authored SVG by Sol.

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. No server, build, dependencies, fonts, accounts, analytics,
network requests, storage, or sound. The single file is the whole piece.

- Move the **Choose a moment** slider; its arrow keys work too.
- **Let it cross** takes 90 seconds from departure to arrival. Pause whenever you want.
- Moving the slider pauses playback. At arrival, **Cross again** starts over.
- Switching to another tab pauses it. Returning does not restart it.
- With the system's **reduced motion** preference, playback is hidden; the slider changes still scenes.
- Without JavaScript, the first illustration and stanza remain readable.

Nothing is measured. Nothing is saved. This is an imagined crossing, not a real route, timetable,
astronomical simulation, or wellness exercise. The midpoint has a cup of tea but no productivity advice.

## Text-only crossing

### Departure

The last house leaves its light on.\
Not for us, necessarily.\
Still, it helps.

### Open water

Out here, the dark has room.\
Even the mountains\
put down their names.

### The middle

Someone has left a cup\
beside the wheel.\
A small, ordinary lighthouse.

### First light

The shore returns in pieces:\
a roof, a birch,\
the blue becoming blue.

### Arrival

No revelation.\
Just a rope thrown neatly\
and someone there to catch it.
